Hermes

Hermes is a personal AI assistant that helps a single user manage their own Google account: Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive.

It is a private, single-user application. It is not offered as a service to anyone else and no other users' data is accessed.

Hermes runs locally on the user's own machine and uses OAuth 2.0 to act on the user's behalf only after explicit consent.

See the Privacy Policy for details.
