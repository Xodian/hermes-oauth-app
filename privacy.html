Privacy Policy for Hermes

Last updated: November 2026

What Hermes is

Hermes is a private, single-user personal assistant application. It is used exclusively by its owner to manage that person's own Google account. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts, sign-ups, or access to anyone else.

What data Hermes accesses

With the user's explicit OAuth consent, Hermes accesses Gmail (read and send), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts (read-only), Google Sheets, and Google Docs, solely to perform actions the user requests, such as summarizing email, scheduling, and organizing files.

Where the data goes

Nowhere. Hermes runs locally on the user's own machine. Messages it reads are processed locally and are never uploaded to, stored on, or shared with any third-party service, server, or database. The OAuth token is stored locally on the user's machine only.

What is collected and shared

Nothing. Hermes collects no analytics, has no advertising, sells nothing, and shares nothing with anyone. There are no third parties involved other than Google's APIs, which the user authorizes directly through Google's own consent screen.

Revoking access

The user can revoke Hermes' access at any time from their Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the GitHub repository hosting this page.
